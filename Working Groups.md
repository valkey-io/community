## Working Groups
Valkey's community work is organized into working groups focused on specific areas of the project. Working groups run their own meetings and mailing lists, and anyone is welcome to join.

### Engagement Working Group
The Engagement Working Group coordinates how the project shows up outside of code: conference talks, blog posts, meetups, and other community-facing content that helps people discover and adopt Valkey.

Mailing list: [engagement-wg@lists.valkey.io](https://lists.valkey.io/g/engagement-wg)
Slack channel: #valkey-engagement-wg
  **To get involved, subscribe to the mailing list above or request access to the Slack channel by asking in #everyone. Slack channels are invite-only.**
